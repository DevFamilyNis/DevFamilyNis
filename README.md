## Current Focus

### EmPay

EmPay is a business system for delivery companies, focused on payroll calculations, courier data management, company structures, partner reports, analytics, and operational control.

The system helps companies manage large numbers of couriers, process partner reports, calculate earnings, track deductions, generate payouts, and get a clear overview of business performance.

**Stack:** Laravel, MySQL, Livewire, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js

---

### FleetManager

FleetManager is a platform for electric bike rental businesses, built for delivery riders and companies that need better control over vehicles, rentals, clients, payments, and operational records.

The system is designed to support both internal administration and public-facing rental workflows.

**Stack:** Laravel, MySQL, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js

---

### Limitless

Limitless is a business management application focused on clients, projects, invoices, expenses, tax years, issue tracking, internal permissions, and operational reporting.

The system is designed to help service-based businesses organize their daily work, track financial records, manage client projects, and keep business operations structured in one place.

**Stack:** Laravel, MySQL, Livewire, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, Flux UI
